2. Deep study of the role direction (note item 3). What we know (re-read from the v4 layer profile and reports):
   - Role vectors were never used for steering. R_id and R_act exist in v3 and v4 (300 per arm, grant + postmortem).
   - Response-mean R_id: split-half cosine -0.55 (mean over layers 6-20), held-out AUC 0.42. R_act: -0.47, 0.44.
     Noise. E in the same readout: split-half 0.18, AUC 0.57, yet E steers. Split-half is not the gate; steering is.
   - Prompt-side readouts (assistant-start, tail3/4) are reliable for R_id and R_act (0.88 to 0.99) but these
     readouts did not steer for E in week 6, so no reason to expect them to for role.
   - cos(E, R_id) = 0.48 to 0.66 at layers 4 to 20; cos(E, R_act) = 0.26 to 0.32.
   - The role effect itself is weak in Qwen3.5-9B: paper 0.57 n.s.; ours 0.55 (31/25) and 8/6.
