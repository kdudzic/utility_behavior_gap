1. Coherence vs score, closer (note item 1). Offline, no cost.
   a. Distributions, not means: histogram of steered-essay coherence per dose at layers 6, 10, 14, 20 against
      unsteered and random. Share of steered essays below 80 and below 70 per cell.
   b. Mixture table per cell: share of pairs "better and as coherent", "damaged" (gap below -3), "no change";
      win rate inside each part. Shows whether the win rate and the coherence loss come from different essays.
   c. Is damage topic-specific? For each of the 30 topics, count how often its steered essay falls below 80
      across layers and doses. If a few topics carry the damage, it is a prompt interaction, not a dose effect.
   d. Dose curve of both shares (better, damaged) by layer. Tells which layer and dose gives gains without the
      damaged tail (layer 6 +0.1 looks like it: 22 / 3 with coherence 89.2 vs 88.7).
   e. Same tables for removal (r < 0), where coherence rises slightly and the win rate falls, the other half
      of the "inverse relation" (Spearman -0.20 over all effort cells, +0.53 and +0.62 within each sign).
